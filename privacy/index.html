Plain-language policy · 28 August 2026

Privacy stays on your side of the screen.

What stays local

Your imported CSV contents, column mapping, balances, receipt index, and license token are stored only in this browser using IndexedDB or localStorage. The app performs parsing, duplicate detection, and reconciliation on your device. We do not upload, view, or sell this financial data.

What leaves the device

The static app may receive ordinary hosting request logs such as an IP address, requested path, browser user agent, and time. We do not add analytics or tracking. If you buy or verify Proof Kit, your browser contacts the Sociobot billing API with the license token. Hosted checkout is provided by Sociobot with Dodo as merchant of record under their applicable policies.

Your controls

Use “Erase local draft” to remove the active statement. Clearing this site’s browser data removes drafts, receipt indexes, and the locally saved license. Download your cleaned CSV, receipt, and draft backup before clearing data if you want to retain them.

Security and limits

Browser storage is not a durable backup and may not be encrypted by this app. Device encryption and browser profile security protect it where your platform provides those controls. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile may be able to access the draft.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the project’s public source repository. This policy changes only when the product’s data behavior changes.
