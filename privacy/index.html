Your bank CSV stays in this browser

What stays local

Your bank CSV, column mapping, balances, and receipts stay in this browser. The app checks repeats and balance gaps on your device.

What leaves the device

The static site receives ordinary hosting requests. We do not add analytics or tracking. Buying or verifying Proof Kit sends a license token to Sociobot billing.

Your controls

Use Erase local draft to remove the active bank CSV. Download a CSV, receipt, or draft backup before clearing browser data.

Security and limits

Browser storage is not a durable backup. Your browser profile and device settings control its protection.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the project’s public source repository.
